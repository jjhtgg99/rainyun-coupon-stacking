## The points mall is where the repeat discounts live

RainYun runs a points currency where 2,000 points equals ¥1, and points convert into either cash withdrawal or vouchers. The voucher section of the mall refreshes on a random schedule and restocks around 20:00 daily, at least during promotional periods, so people set alarms for it.

Earning points is mostly grunt work plus one genuinely clever trick:

- Daily check-in: **500 points** per day, repeatable. Over a month that's 15,000 points, or ¥7.50 of value, for clicking a button.
- Bind email: 1,000 points, one time.
- Bind WeChat: 2,000 points, one time.
- Join the official QQ group: 1,500 points, one time.
- New-user tasks in total: up to roughly 8,500 points, one time.
- Spending on products: 10% cashback in points by default, rising to **12% once you've spent ¥1**. That jump is the trick — buy the cheapest possible thing first, and every later order earns at the higher rate. On an ¥80 server that's 19,200 points instead of 16,000.
- Referrals: 2,000 points per signup, plus 10,000 more if your referral actually buys something.

The mall's voucher stock rotates, and the ceiling is documented at around ¥20 face value for a single voucher. Stock is genuinely limited — during RainYun's spring campaigns the 50%-off game-cloud vouchers and ¥50-off direct vouchers sold out and restocked nightly.

If you don't feel like waiting on mall rotation, RainYun Ⅱ-level members and above can also *gift* vouchers to another user, spending their own points at 1,000 points per ¥1 of voucher value. That's the channel the 6.5折 vouchers come through.

## Which region makes sense for which job

The pricing table only tells you half the story; the network routing is the other half, and reviewers have tested most of these nodes.

- **Mainland business that needs speed**: Suqian or Shiyan. Suqian's Platinum line is unlimited-traffic with 20G+–150G DDoS mitigation, and it's the cheapest per gigabyte on the list. Tencent BGP and Telecom lines in Shenzhen cost more but carry the heaviest mitigation.
- **Serving mainland users without an ICP filing**: Hong Kong Zone 2. Reviewers measured three-network direct return routing (CN2 for Telecom, CMI for Mobile, CU for Unicom) with average latency in the 56ms range and sub-35ms from Shanghai.
- **Bandwidth-hungry work — downloads, media proxies, game servers for overseas players**: Hong Kong Zone 5. Independent testing hit 485Mbps download and 365Mbps upload against a 500Mbps plan, with a mixed Hong Kong IP that unlocks Netflix, Disney+, and ChatGPT but not TikTok.
- **Cross-border e-commerce and social media**: Korea Seoul Zone 1. This is the line where RainYun offers genuine residential IPs at ¥25/month, and the tester confirmed native IP checks against TikTok, Netflix, and ChatGPT. Return routing runs CN2/4837/CMI.
- **Raw single-core performance**: the Zhenjiang i9-14900K game cloud line. Benchmarks put it at 4,871 single-thread in Sysbench against 19,320 multi-thread — that ratio is the whole point for game servers.

Two things to keep straight before you buy. Mainland regions require an ICP filing plus whitelisting before a domain resolves on standard HTTP ports; Hong Kong, Korea, and other overseas regions don't. And NAT plans, which is what the Suqian pricing above represents, share a public IP with other customers and give you a fixed number of port mappings — fine for a private game server, wrong for anything that needs its own address. Shenzhen and Suqian both let you add a dedicated IP for a fee.

## RainYun coupon FAQ

**Does the 50% coupon apply to renewals?** No. It's classified as a new-purchase coupon. Renewal vouchers exist but are shallower.

**Can I use a coupon and the annual discount together?** Yes. That's the combination RainYun's own partner content advertises at 5.6折, and forums document the same behavior.

**What's the coupon's validity window?** It varies by campaign. During RainYun's spring event, coupons issued during the promotion stayed valid for a month. Check the expiry printed on the voucher itself rather than assuming.

**Are any coupons restricted by region?** Occasionally. An older points-mall discount coupon carried an explicit Hubei exclusion in its terms, so read the terms before you count on a percentage.

**What happens if I refund?** RainYun offers no-reason refunds within seven days, limited to three per account, with the money returned to your RainYun balance. Vouchers and points you consumed on that order do not come back.

**Is there a genuinely free option?** Yes, for game cloud. RainYun lets you redeem a free game cloud server with points and renew it with points, with 2C/4GB configs among the options. Stock is limited and typically restocks around 20:00. It's a smaller box on shared resources, but daily check-ins alone earn 500 points, so the economics work if you're patient.

**How do I test before committing?** Cloud servers and game cloud support a ¥1 one-day trial, limited to once per product line and once per region per week. According to the documented stacking guides, buying the trial counts as a purchase rather than a renewal, so a new-purchase coupon still applies afterward when you buy the full term. Trial instances get reclaimed within about 12 hours of expiring, so don't keep anything on one you'd miss.
